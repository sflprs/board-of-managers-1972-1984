The following were discussed: A request to increase the 160 meals to 200
for the Chinese Self Help program has been received. Mr. Mann listed the
CAHA and AAHA meetings for the near future. The Resident party for March 13
will use the theme, "Two Hundred Years of Pioneers." Residents needing more
care will be moved either into or near the Health Center. Brochures are
being prepared. Though not an epidemic, several of the Staff and Residents

have been stricken with influenza. The Heritage insurance is being reviewed.

The January budget has given no evidence of serious problems.
