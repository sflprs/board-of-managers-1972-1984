ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT:

A copy of the Administrator's Report will be attached to these minutes.

In brief, the medical and utility costs are increasing, and travel costs
were higher in April due to lack of transportation during the strike.

The C.A.H.A. Annual Meeting was held in San Francisco this month. Uniform
Accounting, an extension of the 1971 Hospital Disclosure Act, has been
expanded to apply to all Health Facilities. The California Health|
Facilities Commission, which now includes two C.A.H.A. representatives,
will oversee the establishment of these regulations. Miss Kathryn
Hendricks will replace Miss Lorraine Anderson as Administrative Secretary.
