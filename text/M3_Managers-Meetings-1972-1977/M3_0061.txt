GARDEN COMMITTEE :

Mrs, Porter reported that the garden has had a major pruning. Ten truck-
loads of prunings were taken away. However, the garden does not look
bare, it looks very nice. Neighbors on Octavia Street have complained

about dropping limbs and other debris.

The Committee has made a survey of the building to determine what is
heeded. There is a need for lamps, shades, pictures, a coffee table, etc.
The first floor, including the Dining Room, needs refurbishing. This will
be considered as a major expenditure in next year's budget.
