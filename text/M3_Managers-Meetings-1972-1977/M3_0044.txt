3. Health Department Inspection of Residential Care Facility.
We were visited by Eleanor Blankensnip of the Department of Public
Health last week on Tuesday and Thursday. She praised us highly
for our fine residence and found only minor discrepancies in
employee and residential records. Corrections are being enacted
now and our license will be renewed in a few days.

Unfortunately, the cost for re-licensure has gone up from $100
in 1975 to about $483 in 1976. Notice, however, that the inspec-
tion just referred to was not the long-awaited Health Center
inspection.
