ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT

Mr. Roger Stevens, the new Administrator of The Heritage since September
1, 1977 told the Board regular staff meeting are planned with the staff
members accountable and the summerized reports will be presented to

the Board.

New staff members include a Bergeron, Charlotte Cole, and

Ritch McParland.

With regret, Mr. Stevens informed the Board of the death of mrs.
Genevieve Blessing, a resident of The Heritage for many years.

Mrs. Ruth Newmann has been returned to the hospjtal.

At Mr. Stevens direction more hours are planned to keep the Health
Center clean. If more efficient employees are indicated, a change
will be made.

A temporary gardener using too much water resulted in a $200.00
fine from the San Francisco Water Department. Mr. Stevens is asking
the Water Department to reconsider.

Mr. Stevens is planning to purchase a poloroid camera to be used as
a legal protection.
