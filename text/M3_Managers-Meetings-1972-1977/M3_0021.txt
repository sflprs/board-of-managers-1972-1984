A copy of the Administrator's Report will be attached to these minutes.

In brief, in January one Resident passed away, no new Residents entered
The Heritage; on January 1 there were 112 Residents and 9 Private Patients,
at the end of the month, 111 Residents and 9 Private Patients. Per capita
cost of care for January was $656.80 + $50; per capita income from
Residents, $287.31 (considering only monthly care fees). The budget
category for Maintenance & Repairs was overexpended because of Bruce
Hiddleson's work on the front doors, which had not been budgeted. The
estimate for the entire job is $1,000. Mr. Mann congratulated Mrs.
Porter, Miss Balcom, and Mrs. McDaniel for their very farsighted effort
regarding the residential rate increases, which will result in fewer

rate increases in the future. The Board of Trustees met on January 17

to consider three bidders for the Health Center study; no decision has
been made, and Mr. Stanford requested bids from two more accounting firms.
Kathryn Hendricks is leaving The Heritage to accept a position at a
considerably higher salary level. Mr. and Mrs. Harry Chen offered their
resignations effective March 1. Marty Geelhoed has also asked to be
replaced, but he given no definite departure date.

Motions were carried to express appreciation to the Chens and to Kathryn
Hendricks for their work.

The Maintenance Department will require approximately $5,000 to effect
changes required by OSHA, the City Fire Marshall, and the State Department
of Public Health (Miss Bridge inquired whether these would be listed as
Capital Expenditures or Repairs. Mr. Mann explained that any new item
costing more than $200 is considered a Capital Expenditure.)

We are attempting water conservation measures and would like to install
such water savers as flow reducers in all Residents' rooms. Printed
conservation information has been disseminated to all Residents.
