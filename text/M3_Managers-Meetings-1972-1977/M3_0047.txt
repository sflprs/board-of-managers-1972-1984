SPECIAL ACTIVITIES:

The Spring Tea was a success. Three concerts were given in April. There
was an all-day bus trip to Monterey. It was estimated that 31% of the
residents take part in the activities at The Heritage. The average for
retirement homes is 18%.

program could be counted upon to continue indefinitely.
