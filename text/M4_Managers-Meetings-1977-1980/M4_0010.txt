Board of Manager's Meeting Page Two
October 28, 1980

There was discussion concerning the decrease in donations and bequests
made to the Heritage and as to what effect this has on the financial
icture. Mrs. McDaniel noted that we need clarification from the

stees as to where the Heritage stands financially today, in 1980,

and what we can look ahead to in 1981 in order to determine whether

a community effort for fundraising is necessary. Mr. Stewens stated

that he has already written a letter to Mr. Morshead, the Chief Finan-

cial Officer of the Board of Trustees for clarification in this area.

Mr. Stevens reported that Mr. Byrdsley, a Heritage Resident, was
struck by a Muni bus last week and is back with us now in the Heritage
Health Center. The Heritage will try to recover from Muni the medical
costs incurred by Mr. Byrdsley as a result of this accident.

Mrs. McDaniel reminded the Board that occupancy in the Health Center is
reaching capacity and that in the event that our Residents should need
the space, it might be necessary to ask private patients to relocate.

Mr. Stevens noted that although the Gay Nineties Party was not
mentioned in the report, it was enjoyed very much by the Residents.
He thanked Mr. & Mrs. Lampen and Mrs. Pinkham for their attendance.

ADMISSIONS COMMITTEE

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Gallatin, Mrs. Avenali) that
Miss LaMetta Ash be accepted as a permanent Resident of the Heritage.

The Admissions Committee is still giving its attention to the "special
needs" couple who are in the process of applying.

Mrs. Abbott, Chairman, noted that the Admissions Committee is meeting
today to discuss the procedure for processing individuals whose names
have reached the top of the waiting list.

CRAFTS COMMITTEE

Mrs. Lampen, Chairman, reported that Miss Newport, Treasurer of the
Resident's Council, called the Board of Equalization to get a ruling
on sales tax for the Heritage Gay Nineties Sale. Because we are a
nonprofit organization, the sale is not open to the general public,
and the sale lasts only for two or three hours, the Heritage is not
required to charge sales tax.

Miss Newport noted the money expended from the Resident's Council
fund as follows:

Employees Appreciation Fund 300.00
Mrs. Mason on Retiring 100.00
Police Athletic League 30.00
Hobby Shop Supplies 65.65

Mrs. Lampen reported that at the October 25th sale, two blankets
and 8 sweaters were sold for a total cost of $89.00. The cost of
the yarn was $16.00, making a net profit of $73.00.

FOOD COMMITTEE

Mrs. Finley, Chairman, noted that both the Columbus Day Party and
the Gay Nineties Party were very well received.

It was suggested that Resident Parties not be held on weekends as it is
difficult for Board members to attend. However, after discussion, it
was determined that occasional parties on weekends are helpful in
breaking up the weekend for Residents.

Mrs. McDaniel noted that the Residents Activities Calendar is available
in the Reception Office and on the bullentin boards on either end of
the Dining Room. She welcomes comments on how the program of activities
might be enriched or enhanced.

Mrs. Wert will begin offering backgammon lessons to the Residents this
week,
