September 1, 1979.

merit increase of $50.00 a month as of August 1, 1979.

CRAFTS
Mrs. Lampen, Chairman, told the Board the Residents are making
tabel mats, crocheted hangers and many other gifts to be offered

for sale at the October Boutique which will be held October

LIASON

Mrs. Sherman, Chairman, commented on the expansion of the Self
Help for the Elderly which if pursued can be financed from
Capital Expenditures.

An architectural study to be used sometime in the future, costing
up to $5,000.00 to utilize any possible space for living quarters
or storage has been approved by the Board of Trustees.

GARDEN

Mrs. Porter, Chairman, is waiting for an estimate of $1300.00 to
remove the Yew tree to be sent to her in writing as she has
requested.

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Sherman, Mrs, Pope) that
Mrs. Porter be authorized to proceed and then report it to the
Finance Committee.
