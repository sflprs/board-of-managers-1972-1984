HOSPITALITY

Mrs. McDaniel announced that we need a Board member to be responsible
for the resident "Teas". Mrs. Vermilya handled the teas last year
and did an excellent job. At this time, a Board member is needed to
be in charge of the September Tea. Mrs. Rhine volunteered to do so.

It was suggested that the residents be approached to see if they would
like to set up the teas in the future.

The Board approved of the efforts being made to use our own kitchen
and waitresses when parties are given at the Heritage.
