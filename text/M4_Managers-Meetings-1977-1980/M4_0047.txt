BOARD OF DIRECTORS' MEETING

November 27, 1979
CALL _TO ORDER
The regular meeting of the Board of Directors of the San Francisco
Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society was called to order at 10:40 a.m.
on November 27, 1979 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco, California,
by the President, Mrs. Knox Finley.
PRESENT.
Miss Balcom, Mesdames Abbott, Avenali, Ferguson, Finley, Forward,
Gallatin, Howard, Lampen, McDaniel, Mitchum, Pinkham, Pope, Porter,
Scott, Stackhouse, Vermilya and Watts.
MINUTES

The Minutes of the previous meeting, October 23, 1979, were approved
as mailed.

PRESIDENT

Mrs. Knox Finley introduced Mrs. Malcolm S. M. Watts, a new member
of the Board of Directors.
