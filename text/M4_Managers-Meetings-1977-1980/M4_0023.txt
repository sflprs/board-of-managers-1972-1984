Board of Managers' Meeting Page Two
June 24, 1980

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT

See distributed report.

The Board is concerned about the amount of time it takes to fill a
room once it is vacated, Mr. Stevens outlined the present procedure
used by the administrative staff. The Admissions Committee is
working on streamlining this process.

The Board asked Mr. Stevens to check other C.A.H.A. residences

to see how many homes have changed, or are in the process of
changing, their provisions on complete health care.

SPECIAL EVENTS

The Board expressed its appreciation to Mrs. Ferguson for her
efforts in making the luncheon on June 17th a great success. Because
the luntheon involved the presentation of the first 100 years of
records for the San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society
to the California Historical Society, it was especially nice to
have former Board members invited.

CRAFTS

No Report.

FOOD

Mrs. Finley, Chairman, reported that Mr. Pontzious is planning a
Fourth of July barbecue for the residents.

GARDEN

No Report.

HEALTH

Mrs. Pope, Chairman, informed the Board that a meeting has been
held with Mrs. Oba, Director of Nursing, and John Killeen, Activities
Director. Mr. Killeen will make a list of people for Health
Committee members to call on.

HOUSE

Mrs. McDaniel stated that a chairman has not yet been appointed for the
House Committee.

LIBRARY

Mrs. Pope, a member of the Library Committee, informed the Board that
donations of magazines are always welcome and very much appreciated.

TRUSTEE MEETING

Mrs. McDaniel reported that she and Mrs. Wert attended the Trustees’
meeting on June 17th in their new roles as Trustees.

The following capital improvement items were passed:
1. Pressure reducing valves on all intake water.
2. Fire doors for the first and second floor Morgan building.
3. A refrigerator and freezer for the kitchen.

The Trustees also voted to raise the rent on the flats. Mrs.
McDaniel was designated Recording Secretary for the Trustees.

ADMISSIONS

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Porter, Miss Balcom) to
accept Miss Margaret Mundy as a permanent resident of the Heritage.
