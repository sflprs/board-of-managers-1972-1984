Board of Managers' Meeting

Page Two
November 25, 1980

The Board of Managers asked Mr. Stevens to refer both the Otis
Elevator bid for the addition of lights indicating whether the
elevator was working and the bid for work on the boilers to the
Trustees for discussion and appropriate action.

EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE

The Heritage has been approached to offer it's facilities, or a
portion thereof, for use as a neighborhood polling place during
the elections. The Board agreed that it would not be appropriate
for the general public to be entering the Resident's "home" for
voting. However, our tenants in the flats on Francisco Street
might wish to offer the use of their garage for this purpose.

The Public Health Department would like to offer a class at the
Heritage which would be open to the general public. The Board
asked Mr. Stevens to get the feeling of the Residents first
regarding their willingness to attend and having outsiders attend
t.

ADMISSIONS COMMITTEE

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Gallatin, Mrs. Cox), to
accept Mrs. Demaris Wood as a permanent restident of the Heritage.

The Board agreed that it would be of benefit to have the names of
individuals to be interviewed by the Admissions Committee read at
the Board meeting preceeding the interview. If Board members have
pertinent information regarding the applicants, they may talk
directly to the Admissions Committee Chairman after the Board
meeting. No discussion of applicants is to take place in the
Board meeting. Accordingly, the Admissions Chairman read the
names of four applicants who will be interviewed shortly.

FOOD COMMITTEE

Mrs. Finley, Chairman, announced that there will be no Food
Committee meeting during the month of December.

ANNOUNCEMENTS

Mrs. McDaniel showed the Board the invitation to all Residents
and Board Members from Mr. & Mrs. Edward Foster to a party on
Sunday, December 14th at 4:00 p.m. in honor of Mr. Everett
Foster's 90th birthday.

Mrs. Cox announced that invitations to the Residents' Christmas
Parton on Wednesday, December 17th at 7:00 p.m. have been sent

out. She would very mich like Board members to sign as hostesses
(approximately 8 are needed). Heritage waitresses will be serving
the refreshments this year.

A special day-long Board meeting, dedicated to long-range planning
for the Heritage. will be scheduled for Tuesday, January 13, 1981
at 9:45 a.m.

Board members are asked to help decorate the Heritage for Christmas
on Monday, December 15th at 10:00 a.m.

ADJOURNMENT

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at
12:10 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,

Wilmer G. Logan
Recording Secretary
