FINANCE COMMITTEE
Miss Balcom, Treasurer, discussed in detail the budget for 1980 to be
presented for approval to the Board of Trustees, December 14, 1979.

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Porter, Mrs. Abbott) to
accept the proposed budget to be presented for approval to the Board
of Trustees.

Mrs. Porter, with enthusiastic agreement of the Board Members,
expressed appreciation of Miss Balcom's fine work on the budget and
for the many hours she devoted to the budget.

CRAFTS

Mrs. Lampen, Chairman, spoke of the October Boutique and its great
success.

GARDEN

Mrs. Porter, reported the 85 veronicas have been replanted as well as
the replacement of the two yews.

HEALTH

Mrs. Oba, Supervisor of the Health Center, suggested bath powder for
the ladies and shaving lotion for the gentlemen would be appropriate
Christmas gifts.

HOUSE

Mrs. Abbott, Chairman, told the Board the House Committee has listed
and designated the artifacts to be appraised. Mr. Samuels and Mr.
Bowman, who was suggested by Mr. Samuels to assist with the fine arts,
examined and appraised the furnishings and fine arts. The Board of
Trustees will decide upon the insurance using the replacement figures
as a guide.

LIBRARY

Mrs. Avenali, Chairman, mentioned books and magazines are always
welcome.

EXECUTIVE

Mr. Stevens informed the Board of the bid received for the planned
Chinese Food Program Expansion. The Board continues to approve and
support this expansion of our community activity.

FOOD

To assist Mr. Greg Pontzious, both to help keep within the budget and
to facilitate service, the Food Committee is eliminating special orders
with only the designated alternative available. These alternatives
available include such substitues as orange juice and cold cereals

for breakfast, beef patties, scrambled eggs and fruit salad for lunch
and ice cream for dessert.

ORIENTATION

Mrs. Gallatin, Chairman, will give Mrs. Watts a tour of the building
today but will schedule an orientation meeting for both Mrs. Watts
and Mrs. Rhine in January. Any Board Member or Trustee wishing to
attend is most welcome.
