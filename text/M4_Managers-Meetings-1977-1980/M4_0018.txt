Board of Managers' Meeting | Page Four
September 23, 1980

ANNOUNCEMENTS

Mrs. McDaniel stated that Marla Long should be informed by the
first Friday of the month of any dates for meetings for Board
committees or activities. These will be included on the schedule
sent with the minutes which will go out on the second Friday of
the month. They will also be listed on the master calendar in
the Reception office.

The Board was reminded that donations of clothing are welcome,
both easy-care bedjackets and dressing-gowns for the Health Center
and Cay atnes for other Residents. These should be brought to
Mrs. Hill.

PRAISE FOR HERITAGE STAFF MEMBERS

Mrs. McDaniel read three letters received in recent weeks: 1) from
Barbara Bowers, nurse practitioner at the Heritage, to the Board

of Managers, praising the skill and dedication of Mrs. Oba, and

the Health Center nurses; 2) from the Thursday Exercise Class,

(a community service of the Heritage), expressing thanks to Mr.
Stevens and the Board, and warmest appreciation of John Killeen;

and 3) from Jan Garnett, Director of Public Relations and Fund
Development for C.A.H.A., complimenting Mr. Stevens on the excellent
content and approach of the August Heritage Happenings,

which is edited by our Activities Assistant, Nancy Garfield.

ADJOURNMENT

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at
12:35 p.m. in memory of Mrs. Ernest Gunther.

Respectfully submitted,

Mrs. Robert Vermilya
Assistant Recording Secretary
in the absence of Mrs. Jack 5. Logan
