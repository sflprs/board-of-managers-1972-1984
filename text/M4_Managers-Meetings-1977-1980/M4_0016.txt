Board of Managers' Meeting Page Two
September 23, 1980

Board members stressed that the Board of Managers should continue
to be consulted on major operational expenditures, even if power
of approval now lies with the Trustees. Mrs. McDaniel commented
that the summer interval is a difficult time for formal handling
of matters requiring prompt action, but that it is the general
wish and intention of Mr. White and the Trustees that the Board
of Managers be consulted in all matters related to their area of
responsibility; however, procedures need to be clarified for all
concerned, and she and Mrs. Avenali will be discussing this with
the Trustees.

The Board agreed that they would like Ms. Henderson, the accountant,
to resume giving the two figure breakdown on the report for the Finance
Committee, to show "Per Capita Cost Plus Depreciation".

A motion to accept the Treasurer's Report as presented was moved,
seconded, and carried. (Mrs. Porter, Mrs. Stackhouse)

Miss Balcom presented a report on revisions of the Finance Committee
Reports for January through March to be added as a statement attached
to the minutes.

CORRESPONDING SECRETARY

Mrs. Watts, Corresponding Secretary began her report by presenting
a list of donations made from the time she has taken office as
follows: 1) a walker was donated by Mrs. George Sikol; 2) furniture
given by Mrs. Burt Hulting, Mrs. Louise Nixon, Captain Girvin Wait
and Miss Lametta Ash; 3) Mrs. David J. McDaniel for the purchase of
corsages and button-hole flowers to honor our resident volunteers;
4) two cases of wine by Mr. & Mrs. Peter Avenali; 5) Mrs. A.M.D. Lampen,
Mrs. Edwin Bruck, Mrs. John W. Gilpin, and Mrs. William H. Nigh in
memory of Mrs. Ernest Gunther; 6) donations by Mr. & Mrs. Walter
Gallatin in memory of Mr. Kenneth C. Dodson and Mr. Thomas Gorman;
and 7) Mrs. Jack S. Logan, Mrs. Walter Schilling, and Miss Jean M.
Gray in memory of Mrs. Donald Craig.

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Avenali, Mrs. Finley) that
the procedure be changed for the handling of donations to the Heritage.
In the future, the procedure will be carried through by the office and
a list of the month's donations mailed to Mrs. Watts at the end of
every month.

Mrs. Wert, First Vice-President, reported that, in the absence of
Mrs. McDaniel, she sent a letter and flowers from the Board to the
family of Mrs. Ernest Gunther. Mrs. McDaniel stated that she had
also sent an arrangement before she left on vacation to Mrs. Gunther.

Mrs. McDaniel read a letter from Tony Mason, expressing how much she
has enjoyed her volunteer work at the Heritage. Mrs. Lampen repre-
sented the Board at a special lunch at the Heritage earlier this
month which honored Mrs. Mason and our two other volunteers from the
community, Mrs. Hall and Mrs. Coombs, who have been working under
John Killeen.

HOUSE COMMITTEE
