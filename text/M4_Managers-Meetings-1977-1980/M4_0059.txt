BOARD OF DIRECTORS' MEETING
July 24, 1979
CALL TO ORDER

The regular meeting of the Board of Directors of the San Francisco
Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society was called to order at

10:40 a.m. on July 24, 1979 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco,
California, by the President, Mrs. Knox Finley.

PRESENT

Miss Balcom, _Mesdames Abbott, Finley, Gallatin, Howard, Lampen,
Logan, McDaniel, Moffitt, Pinkham, Pope, Porter, Sherman, Stackhouse,
and Vermilya. Mr. Roger Stevens, Administrator.

MINUTES

The Minutes of the regular Board Meeting, June 26, 1979, were
approved as mailed.

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT
See distributed copy.

(See Health Center) Mr. Stevens also informed the Board the
bathroom floors and the main shower in the Health Center have been

retiled.

Miss Elizabeth Phillips is moving to the first floor and Mrs. Etta
Dakin has been admitted permanently to the Health Center. Miss
Phillips vacated room will be available soon.

A discussion ensued concerning Residents with walkers using Morgan
Hall. The Resident Council recommended such persons be discouraged
from remaining any length of time in Morgan Hall. Mrs. Abbott, as
a Board Member, expressed her disagreement with the Resident Council
recommendation.

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Sherman, Mrs. Lampen) that
Mr. Stevens should continue to handle this situation.

The possible expansion of the Self Help for the Elderly Program
(Chinese Food Program) was discussed. A letter of intent was sent

to the Self Help for the Elderly a week ago. It was moved,

seconded and carried (Mrs. Logan, Mrs. Pope) that Mr. Stevens

be authorized to proceed with this proposal of expansion as presented
to the Executive Council of the Board of Directors and encouraged by
the Board of Trustees. It was further approved that two Board
Members would assist Mr. Stevens with this expansion.
