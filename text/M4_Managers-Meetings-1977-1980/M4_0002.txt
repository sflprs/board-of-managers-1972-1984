TREASURER'S REPORT

Mrs. Avenali, Treasurer, presented the Report of the Finance Comittee
for the month of October, 1980. (See distributed report). There was be
discussion as to exactly what constituted the "other income’! category. \
Mrs. Avenali will look into this.

Mrs. Avenali noted that the figures are a little higher for the month
of October because it is a five week month. Also, nurses salaries
and large medical bills, which will for the most part be reimbursed,
have raised the totals.

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Stackhouse, Mrs. Logan), to
accept the Treasurer's Report as presented.

Mrs. Avenali presented the 1981 Budget to the Board of Managers for
approval. She explained that there is a 15% increase in the total
budget and income has been estimated conservatively. It was moved,
seconded and carried (Mrs. Porter, Mrs. Stackhouse) that the 1981 Budget
be approved, subject to final revisions by the Finance Committee and
the Board of Trustees. Miss Balcom abstained from voting.
