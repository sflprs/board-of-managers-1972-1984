BOARD OF MANAGERS' MEETING
October 28, 1980
CALL TO ORDER

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco
Ladies" Protection and Relief Society was called to order at

10:35 a.m. on October 28, 1980 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco,
California by the President, Mrs. David J. McDaniel.

BOARD MEMBERS PRESENT

Miss Balcom, Mesdames Abbott, Avenali, Finley, Forward, Gallatin,
Lampen, Logan, McDaniel, Nishkian, Pinkham, Pope, Porter, Sherman,
Vermilya, Watts, and Wert.

BOARD MEMBERS ABSENT
Mesdames Cox, Ferguson, Mitchum, Rhine, Scott and Stackhouse.
STAFF PRESENT

Mr. Roger Stevens, Administrator, and Ms. Marla Long, Administrative
Assistant.

MINUTES

A motion was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Pope, Mrs. Finley)
to approve the Minutes of the previous meeting as mailed.

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Sherman, Mrs. Lampen) to
approve the attached report on the omissions from the Minutes of the
July 22nd meeting of the Board of Managers as presented by Mrs. Logan.

CORRESPONDING SECRETARY

Mrs. McDaniel suggested to Mrs. Watts, Corresponding Secretary,
that she obtain a list at the end of the month from the office
stating the donations that had been made. This could in turn
be read at the monthly Board meeting to inform members of the
latest donations.

TREASURER'S REPORT

Mrs. Avenali, Treasurer, presented the Treasurer's Report of
the Finance Committee for the month of September, 1980 (See
distributed report). She drew attention to several very large
expenses listed under'Notes to Report". These items have caused
us to be over budget for this month.

Mrs. Avenali informed the Board that the Finance Committee is
noting with concern that the costs connected with the flats seem
to be very high.

Miss Balcom expressed concern over the high level of expenses.

Mrs. Avenali reported that the Finance Committee approved a bid
submitted by the House Committee in the amount of 81,528.00 for
curtains in the seoond floor Morgan building sitting room and
black out blinds for the Friendship Room in the Health Center. The
fabrics have been ordered.

A motion was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Pinkham, Mrs. Finley)
to accept the Treasurer's Report as presented.

Mrs. Avenali closed with noting that the first draft of the budget
has been completed.

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT

See distributed report.

The Board noted with regret the deaths of Miss Gilberte Roustand and
Mrs. Edith White.
