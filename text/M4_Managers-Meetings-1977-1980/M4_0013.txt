Omissions from the Minutes of the July 22nd meeting of the
~ Board of Managers

CORRESPONDENCE

Mrs. McDaniel read a letter of resignation from Mrs. Oscar Sutro.
It was moved, seconded and carried to accept her resignation with

regret.

Mrs. McDaniel also read letters of appreciation in connection with
the presentation of Heritage records to the California Historical
Society and the luncheon marking that occasion: from Natlie Cowan,
Head Librarian of the Society, and from Mrs. Charles Smith and

Mrs. Philip Landis. She also read a letter from Miss Margaret Hall
thanking the Board for their letter of sympathy on the death of
her aunt, Mrs. Donald Craig.
