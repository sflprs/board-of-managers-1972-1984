While the report appears to be satisfactory, there are several items that deserve
serious consideration. Income from dividends was down in February, as was to be
expected. Medical cost reimbursements are low, due to deductible of $60 required
for all Medicare patients which must be met each year. Expenses also were down,
but it must be remembered that February had only 28 days, while January had 31.
This is particularly true of food expenses, which were $11,051 in January and ”
$10,236 in February; however, when prorated to the days in each month, food cost
per day in January were $356.50, while in February they were $365--an increase

of 2.5%. An article in the Chronicle on March 9, stated that food costs in
February had increased 1.6%.
