BOARD OF MANAGERS' MEETING
September 23, 1980

CALL_TO ORDER

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco
Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society was called to order at

10:30 a.m. on September 23, 1980 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco,
California by the President, Mrs. David J. McDaniel.

BOARD MEMBERS PRESENT

Miss Balcom, Mesdames Abbott, Avenali, Cox, Ferguson, Finley,
Forward, Gallatin, Lampen, McDaniel, Porter, Scott, Stackhouse,
Vermilya, Watts, and Wert.

BOARD MEMBERS ABSENT

Mesdames Logan, Mitchum, Nishkian, Pinkham, Pope, Rhine and
Sherman.

STAFF PRESENT

Mr. Roger Stevens, Administrator, and Ms. Marla Long, Administrative
Assistant.

TREASURER'S REPORT

Mrs. McDaniel prefaced the Treasurer's Report by asking Board
members to be sure to take their copies of the report from

the room at the end of the meeting. She stated that reports
would be mailed along with the minutes to absent Board members.

Mrs. Avenali, Treasurer, presented the Treasurer's Report of
the Finance Committee for the month of August, 1980. (See
distributed report).

Mrs. Avenali also reported that the following items were approved
at the August meeting of the Finance Committee:

1. That a recommendation be made to the Board to raise the price
of holiday guest meals from $4.00 to $5.00 beginning with
Thanksgiving.

A request from the Garden Committee to:1) accept a bid of
$2,750.00 for the pruning of trees and hedges; and 2) to

contract for apvayine following the pruning with the understanding
that the cost may exceed the budgeted amount.

3. An increase in salary for Mr. Pontzious until January.
(See Administrator's Report)

4. The hiring of a new crafts teacher to replace the current art
teacher. The salary will be within the amount budgeted for
in the Activities budget.

The Trustees approved the following:

1. Rate increases for Health Center private peel enes from $40 to
$45 per day for four-bed rooms and from $45 to $50 for two-bed
rooms.

2. Wage increase of $1.00 per hour for all nurses and aides in the
Health Center.
