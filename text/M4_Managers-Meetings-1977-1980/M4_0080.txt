Expenses this month were exceptionally high--per capita cost before depreciation
was 13 3/4% higher than in February. This is due to several factors:

1. Increase cost in food purchases, partly because an extra supply was
purchased in anticipation of the Teamsters' strike.
2. Increase in utilities.
3. Especially high medical bills: a) several residents require expensive
radiation treatments, b) two Residents had to have hip surgery, c) three
Residents had lengthy hospital stays.
4. Repairs and maintenance continue to be high and way over budget. 100%
in Food Services, due to necessity to buy additional dishes, and 124%
in Housekeeping, Buildings and Grounds. It behooves all of us to conserve
wherever we can and to encourage the Staff and Residents to do likewise.
