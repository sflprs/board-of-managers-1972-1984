Board of Managers' Meeting Page Three
September 23, 1980

Mr. Stevens informed the Board that the work being done to provide
more non-ambulatory rooms has been completed with the exception of
a petition between the Doctor's office and the second floor. Due
to a misunderstanding between the contractor and the Fire Inspector
this step was overlooked. Our State Inspector, Mary Ballard, will
be here on the 30th of September and may be looking at our progress.

Mr. Stevens explained that ONLOC, a national health organization,
would like to do a study in which our Health Center Residents
would participate. After discussion, a motion was moved, seconded
and carried (Mrs. Avenali, Mrs. Abbott) that the Health Center
Residents be allowed to participate in the study provided that’

it is on a voluntary basis and that confidentiality is respected.

Mr. Stevens again stressed the need for volunteers who are willing
to escort residents to doctor appointments.

CRAFT COMMITTEE

Mrs. Lampen, Chairman, informed the Board that the new crafts teacher,

Mrs. Wagner, was working out wonderfully with 10-12 Residents attending
on Thursdays. The group will be contributing decorations for the

Gay Nineties party which will be held on the last Saturday in October.

Ges ere offered the use of a Gay 90's outfit that would fit a small
esident.

It_was discussed that if items made by the Residents are offered for
sale it should be clearly understood what the money will be spent for.
Mrs. Lampen will get a report from Miss Newport, the Treasurer of

the Resident Council.

HOSPITALITY COMMITTEE

Mrs. Vermilya announced that there will be Teas for Residents in
October, April and May. The attendance of a good number of Board
members is essential, and a sign-up sheet will be circulated.

ADMISSIONS COMMITTEE

It was moved, seconded and carried (Miss Balcom, Mrs. Finiry) to
accept Mr. Ervin Scharff and Miss Mary S. Newman as permanent
Residents of the Heritage.

Mrs. Hulting, Room 330, has moved to Room 240. We have a couple
who will be taking Room 330 pending the results of their physical
examination.

The question; of what statement .‘would be made to applicants on the
policy of age limitation was raised and discussed. It was agreed
to refer this question to the Admissions Committee for clarification.

GARDEN COMMITTEE

Mrs. Porter, Chairman, reported that the sprayers are coming on
Thursday, September 25th. As discussed with the Finance Committee,
the cost may go over the budget, but every effort will be made to
keep within it.

HEALTH COMMITTEE

Mrs. McDaniel reported for Mrs. Pope that members of the Health
Committee should see John Killeen about their visiting assignments.

LIBRARY COMMITTEE

At the request of Mrs. Pope and Mrs. Radcliffe, Mrs. McDaniel reminded
Board members that magazines are always welcome and needed. Issues do
not have to be the most current.
