ACTIVITIES

Mrs. Cox, Chairman, told the Board she had received letters and
messages in appreciation of the Saint Patrick's Day party for
the residents. Young dancers performed and a men's octet made up

of residents, personnel and the administrator, Mr. Roger Stevens,
sang Irish songs. Cake and punch were served.

ADMISSIONS

Miss Margaret Mundy is scheduled to move into the Heritage, March
28, 1980. She will be occupying Room 407.

Two applicants were interviewed in February.

Mrs. Finley, Miss Balcom and Mrs. McDaniel met with Dr. Henderson
to review and clarify health requirements for admission.
