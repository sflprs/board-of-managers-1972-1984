HEALTH COMMITTEE

Mrs. Pope, Chairman, reported that both Mrs. Oba and John Killeen
are pleased about the blackout curtains. Mrs. Oba reported that the
Health Center could use some small chairs with arms for patients to
use in their rooms. She would like 14 but would be happy with 10.
Mrs. McDaniel stated that it would be appropriate to refer this
request to the House Committee but that it was important that the
Health center’staff be involved in the selection so that we are sure
the chairs meét the needs of the Health Center Residents. Mrs.
Porter suggested that perhaps Mr. Scharff or another Resident would
like to make a donation toward the purchase 6f the chairs. It was
moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Forward, Mrs. Vermilya) that the
House Committee work with the Health Committee to bring to the Board
an estimate of cost and recommendation of selection for the chairs
for the Health Center.

The Christmas Party for the Health Center will be at 2:00 p.m. on
December 18th, the day after the Resident's Christmas Party. This
year, Board members are asked to come at 1:00 p.m. to assist.

Mrs. Finley and Mrs. McDaniel attended the Resident's Council Meeting.

ANNOUNCEMENTS

Mrs. McDaniel announced that donations for bingo prizes are always
appreciated.

The deadline for donations for the Employees Appreciation Fund is the
first week of December.

Mrs. McDaniel expressed her desire to hold a special meeting of the
Board to be devoted to long range planning concerning the future of

expressed interest in this idea and such a meeting will be scheduled
possibly for an entire day, sometime in January.
