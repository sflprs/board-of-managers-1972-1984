Board of Managers' Meeting Page Two
May 27, 1980

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT
See distributed report.

The Finance Committee met and approved expenditures for the luncheon
on June 17th, expenditures for new Health Center mattresses, and acted
on an item of wages. The Board stressed quality of the mattresses
should not be sacrificed for a lower price.

Litigation arising from Mrs, Axley's termination has been settled
before going to a formal hearing. The settlement was paid out of
"Legal Fees" in the Budget instead of salaries.

HEALTH

Mrs. Pope, Chairman, stated that John Killeen has been on vacation for
the last couple of weeks and Nancy Garfield has taken over in his
absence and done very well.

Rose Dunwell has taken a turn for the worse.

Again, Mrs. Pope spoke of the regular visits her committee members
make in the Health Center.

GARDEN

Mrs. Porter, Chairman, reported a gift from Mrs. Henderson, a resident,
of two fushias. The Board asked the Corresponding Secretary to write
a letter of thanks to Mrs. Henderson. It was also suggested that the
gift be mentioned in the Heritage Happenings.

Impaciens have been planted in the planter boxes and the petunias are
in bloom.
