April 22, 1980 Bool we

The regular meeting of the Board of Directors of the San Francisco
Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society was called to order at 10:35 a.m.
on April 22, 1980 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco, California by
the President, Mrs. Knox Finley.

PRESENT

CALL _TO ORDER

Miss Balcom, Mesdames Avenali, Ferguson, Finley, Forward, Gallatin,
Howard, Lampen, Logan, McDaniel, Pope, Porter, Scott, Rhine,
Stackhouse, Vermilya, Wert and Watts. Mr. Roger Stevens, Administrator.

MINUTES

The Minutes of the previous regular Board meeting, March 25, 1980
were approved and accepted as mailed.

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT

See distributed report.

Mr. Stevens asked the Board for donations of gifts for the bingo
games and of clothing for the Resident's Clothing Closet, a closet
of gift clothing which Residents may choose clothing for their own
use. It was stressed, this closet is for Residents, not for Staff.
