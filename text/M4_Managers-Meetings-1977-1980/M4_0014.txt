NOTES TO REPORT

Again this month Salaries and Wages are over budget.
Because of the Health Center increase, this category
will probably be $ 20,000 over by the end of the year.

Housekeeping, Equipment Maintenance and Repair is $ 1,830
over budget for the month. Western Automation rebuilt

the washer for $ 1,600, Golden Gate Carpet instal led (resiDenT)
carpet for $ 312 , Sexauer did bath and sink repairs

for § 485, also in the same Dept., Grounds and Gardens

has an expense of $ 2,750 to Fred Taylor for pruning.

In General Administration a legal bill of $ 1700 (reduced
from $ 3000) is the largest over budget item. Outside
Services is over budget this month primarily due to spending

$ 259 on Newspaper Ads. So far this year $ 1064 has been
spent on personnel advertising. Travel and Conferences
includes payments for two special conferences, totaling $ 250.
The Minc. Category includes $ 494 spent on residents and
employees Physicals. A State inspector visited Sept. 29

to see that our personnel files were up to date.

There was also a capital expenditure of $ 5,656 paid
to Servadei Plumbing for two pressure relief valves.
