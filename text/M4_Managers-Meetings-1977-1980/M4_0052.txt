Board of Directors' Meeting Page Two
October 23, 1979

ADMISSIONS

Mrs. Scott, Chairman, told the Board one applicant for residency
had been interviewed in the last month.

Mrs. Merle Sears will become a resident of the Heritage at the end of
this month. One room is available and one apartment is available.

CRAFTS

The October Boutique is to be held this afternoon at 2:00 p.m.
Residents exhibiting have been pricing and labeling the items
for sale. Punch, wine, cake and cookies will be served.

GARDEN

Mrs. Porter told the Board the landscape architect is consulted
when major changes in the garden are planned. The ten foot
Leland cypress trees replacing the two yew trees will be planted
this Saturday. The choice is in keeping with Julia Morgan's plan
when she designed Morgan Hall.

The Board expressed their appreciation of Julia Porter who has
contributed so much to the beauty of the Heritage gardens.

HEALTH

Mrs. Pope reported that each Health Committee member would
"adopt" eight patients in the Health Center.

HOUSE

Mrs. Abbott, Chairman, spoke of the detailed preparation of the
inventory of valuable items belonging to the Heritage. Mr. Ted
Samuels will appraise these items October 29, 1979 so the insurance
may be updated.

The Trustees have requested an inventory of the non-valuable items
as well.
