Under budget and operating at a loss seems to be a contradiction.
We were under budget in expenditures this month $3,449.00, and
$5,287.00 in January, totalling $8,736.00 for the year. However,
our estimates of revenue in the budget were much higher than those
received to date, actually, $22,741.00 less than anticipated.

This is due to the following: 1B monthly care fees are low because
we had only 112 residents instead of the usual 115; 2) accommodation
fees are lower because residents are one year older and we have few
new residents to compensate; 3)medical reimbursements are down
because residents have not yet met the deductible required by
Medicare; and 4) Dividends are low in the month of February.

These facts are a strong indication that we are in a very tight
budget this year and every department must be alert to conserve
whenever possible.

Our main concern, at this time, is that the salaries in the
administration are over budget. This is due to inefficiencies

and countless overtime hours in the book-keeping department.

Steps are being taken to correct this. Also the appraisal of
furnishings in the building, other than antiques, has cost $1,780.00,
an item authorized by the Trustees but not anticipated in the
budget.

I must remind everyone that any expenditures of over $250.00,
whether or not budgeted, must be approved by the Finance Committee.

Respectfully submitted,

Olive G. Balcom
