ADMISSIONS

Mrs. Scott, Chairman, told the Board a double apartment will be available
soon. The committee will meet Thursday, June 28, to interview possible appli-
cants for single or double apartments.

Mr. Howard King moved into The Heritage on June 5, 1979.

CRAFTS

The Board approved the suggestions of Mrs. Lampen, Chairman, to schedule a
Boutique for October 23, 1979 to display and sell goods made in the Craft
Shop. The possibility of an informal afternoon tea was discussed.

The plan will be presented to the Residents! Council for approval,

GARDEN

Mrs. Porter, Chairman, reported 3 bids are being sought for the removal of the
dead yew tree and the other dying yew tree. It was suggested replacing them
with two Leland Cypress trees would maintain the line and proportion envisioned
by the architect, Miss Julia Morgan.

HOUSE

Mrs. Abbott, Chairman, urged the Board Members to visit the Health Center to
see the lovely prints Mrs, Wert has given The Heritage.

A card file on all the House projects, to date in the last years, is being
prepared and will be added to as new projects are completed.

LIAISON

It was moved, seconded and carried (Miss Balcom, Mrs. Pope) to inform the
Board of Trustees that the Board of Directors does not think it wise to proceed

with the plans for the gazebo as a recreation and activity area, or to make any
planned expansion to the present Heritage property.

FOOD

The Food Committee, chaired by Mrs. Logan, will meet July 19, 1979. The
Resident Food Committee and Mrs. Wait will be invited to attend the second
half of the meeting.

POLICY AND PROCEDURE

Mrs. McDaniel, Chairman, reminded Committee Chairmen to check with Mr. Stevens
to verify the amount of the remaining funds in their Committee budgets for 1979.

Committee Chairmen should be prepared to recommend an itemized budget for their
committees for 1980 and to discuss these figures with Mr, Stevens in Septem-
ber 1979, when he prepares the first draft of the operational budget for pre-
sentation to the Finance Committee.
