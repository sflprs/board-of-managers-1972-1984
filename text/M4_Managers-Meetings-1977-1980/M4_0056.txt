Board of Directors' Meeting Page Two
September 25, 1979 7

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Logan, Mrs. Pope) to
accept the Treasurer's Report for June, July and August.

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT

See distributed report.

Mrs. Howard suggested graphic signs at the elevator entrances to
remind Residents that elevators must not be used when the fire
alarm has been sounded.

CORRESPONDING SECRETARY

Mrs. Howard, Corresponding Secretary, wrote Mrs. Byron Nishkian
July 27, 1979 asking her to consider a six month or one year leave
of absense rather than resigning from the Board of Directors.

In July, a note was sent to Miss Marie L. Burgess, thanking her
for her gifts to the Heritage of a myrtle wood bowl and a pair

of book ends.

On September 17, 1979, a note was sent to Miss Helen Bridge asking
her to consider a leave of absence rather than resigning from the
Board of Directors.

ADMISSIONS

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Porter, Mrs. McDaniel)
to accept Mr. & Mrs. John Cantor, Dr. and Mrs. Walter Pelkan and
Mr. Howard King as permanent Residents of the Heritage.

CRAFTS

Mrs. Lampen told the Board the October Boutique is scheduled

for 2:00 p.m. thus assuring an attractive, representative display
for the opening of the Boutique.

GARDEN

Mrs. Porter reported $2,100 will cover the pruning.

HEALTH

Mrs. Pope would like volunteers from the Board to "adopt" a
Resident in the Health Center. This would require regular visits
and individual attention. Mrs. Oba stressed visits should be
scheduled after 1:00 p.m.

NOMINATING

Names of possible directors for the Board of Directors were
submitted. Mrs. Donlon, Chairman, will get in touch with those
suggested,

HOSPITALITY

Mrs. Sherman, Chairman, could not be present and has requested
a three month leave of absence.

The Christmas party is planned for the evening of December 18, 1979.
Mrs. Cox has selected a new artist for the program and also a
different caterer.

FOOD

Mrs. Logan told the Board both the Residents and the Committee
are pleased with the meals now.

POLICY AND PROCEDURE

Mrs. McDaniel reported the Resident Manual is ready for distribution.

OLD BUSINESS
