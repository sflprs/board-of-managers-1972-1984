BOARD OF DIRECTORS' MEETING
October 23, 1979
CALL _TO ORDER

The regular meeting of the Board of Directors of the San Francisco
Ladies" Protection and Relief Society was called to order at

10:40 a.m. on October 23, 1979 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco,
California, by the President, Mrs. Knox Finley.

PRESENT

Miss Balcon, Mesdames Abbott, Donlon, Finley, Forward, Gallatin,
Lampen, McDaniel, Mitchum, Moffitt, Pinkham, Pope, Porter, Scott,
Stackhouse, Vermilya and Wert. Mr. Roget! Stevens, Administrator.

MINUTES

The Minutes of the previous regular Board Meeting, September 25, 1979,
were approved with the following correction. The Admissions report
should read: It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Porter,

Mrs. McDaniel) to accept Mr. and Mrs. Walter Cantor, Dr. and Mrs.
Karl Pelkan and Mr. Howard King as permanent residents of the
Heritage.

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT
Also see distributed copy.

It was with regret the Board of Directors were told of the death of
Miss Elizabeth Ladd.

It was the consensus of the Board that any Board of Director on
leave of absence should receive a copy of the minutes of each
meeting.

CORRESPONDING SECRETARY
Mrs. Finley read a report from Mrs. Howard, Corresponding Secretary:

A note was sent to Mrs. J.J. Weger thanking her for her donation to
the Heritage in memory of Mrs. Ruth Campbell. A letter was sent to
Mrs. A. Barlow Ferguson thanking her for her gift to the Heritage
of three silver trays. A note to Mr. and Mrs. John Arnold Pope
was sent to thank them for their donation to the Heritage in
memory of Robert and Annie Trevanion Hugo-Smith.
