Letters of appreciation from Residents, Mrs. Marie Harris and Mrs. Gladys
Buehle, were read, expressing their pleasure in the Golden Gate Park bus
tours which included viewing the floral plaque celebrating the 125th
Anniversary of the Ladies' Protection and Relief Society,

ANNIVERSARY PARTY COMMITTEE

Mrs. Nishkian reviewed projected costs and discussed final details for the
125th Anniversary Pary, May 1, 1978.

Several donations have been received for the celebration including the
following:

Floral decorations from Mrs. Howard; invitations at a very low figure
from Hoefer, Dieterich and Brown, Inc. Mrs. Ferguson and her mother,
Mrs, Poetz, are providing four musical programs for the month of May and
a harpist for the Anniversary Party. Dr. and Mrs. Sherman are giving a
guest book and a photograph album to be used for the 125th Anniversary
Scrap Book, and there will be free newspaper publicity.

Mrs. Pinkham reviewed the serving details.

Mrs, Roe is in charge of guest tours of The Heritage. Mrs. Roe urged any
Director wishing to take guests on a tour herself, obtain from her a copy
of the tour maps which will indicate Resident rooms open to guests and

the direction the tour should be taken.
