BOARD OF MANAGERS' MEETING
June 24, 1980
CALL TO ORDER

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco
Ladies" Protection and Relief Society was called to order at 10:30
A.M. on June 24, 1980 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco, California
by the President, Mrs. David J. McDaniel.

BOARD MEMBERS PRESENT

Miss Balcom, Mesdames Ferguson, Finley, Gallatin, Logan, McDaniel,
Pinkham, Pope, Porter, Scott, Rhine, Stackhouse, Vermilya and Watts.

STAFF PRESENT

Mr. Roger Stevens, Administrator, and Ms. Marla Long, Administrative
Assistant.

MINUTES

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Logan, Mrs. Pinkham) that
the Minutes of the previous regular Board meeting, May 27, 1980,

be accepted as mailed with the following correction: The Treasurer's
Report should read "per capita (loss) rather than "per capita income
in excess of expenses".

Also recognized was a possible discrepancy in the figure reported
for the "Under the Budget for the Year'' category. It was decided
that the Finance Committee would look into this and report next
meeting.

A discussion was held concerning salary adjustments made during the
year after the budget is passed. The Board noted that if salary
adjustments are within the budget and within the approved salary
range, the decision can be made by the Finance Committee. If it

is over budget and outside the salary range, the Trustees must make
the decision.
