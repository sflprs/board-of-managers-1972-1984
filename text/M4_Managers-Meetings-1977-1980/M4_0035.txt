BOARD OF DIRECTORS' MEETING
March 25, 1980

CALL _TO ORDER
The regular meeting of the Board of Directors of the San Francisco
Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society was called to order at
10:40 a.m. on March 25, 1980 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco,
California by the President, Mrs. Knox Finley.
PRESENT
Mesdames Abbott, Cox, Finley, Forward, Gallatin, Lampen, McDaniel,
Pope, Porter, Scott, Rhine, Stackhouse and Vermilya. Mr. Roger
Stevens, Administrator.
MINUTES
The Minutes of the previous regular Board meeting, February 26, 1980
and the report of the Executive Committee action of March 10, 1980
were approved and accepted as mailed.
ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT
See distributed report.

by Mrs. Ballard. For detail see the report.

room may be repainted at the discretion of the housekeeper.
