NOTES TO REPORT

1, UTILITIES- an overall increase in utility rates accounts for
the continnuing excess over budget. The total excess of $920.00
for May is actually less than the April figure of $945 over budget.
The meter was misread this month, almost doubling the bill.
We called for another reading, which was done on 6/6/80.
The reading was much lower. $1500 was accrued to this account
to minimize the distortion to the budget. During the summer
months utility bills may decline.

2, FEES- the excess over budget of $11,176 is actually an improvement
over the April excess of $18,996. Part of this is due to the
annualizing of the accounting fees budget in May. A final bill
was paid to Coopers & Lybrand for $2,190. The accounting fees
budget is now $44 over budget for the year, however $1,250 is
accrued expense from 1979 written off this year.

Legal fees- $2,500 was paid to G. Axley by order of the
State Labor Commission. Our lawyers were also paid $668.
Other Professional fees is also over budget by $6,721.
This 1S primarily due to the March expenditure of $4,864
to Sprankle, Lynde & Sprague for a feasibility study.
