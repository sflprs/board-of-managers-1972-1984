RAFT COMMITTEE

rs. Lampen reviewed the work of the Craft Shop, which meets every Thursday. Mrs.
ampen is assisted by Mrs. Toni Mason, a volunteer. During the year, 36 blankets,
6 sweaters and 6 hooked rugs were completed in the Craft Shop. The blankets and
weaters were made for the Volunteer Services Program of the Department of Social
ervices, and the Craft Shop is reimbursed for the cost of the yarn. Also, table
ats, table decorations and tray favors are made in the Shop, and several mornings
n the year are devoted to the mending of clothing and blankets for the Residents
n the Health Center, as well as helping a few of the Residents in the Center with
heir knitting and crocheting.
