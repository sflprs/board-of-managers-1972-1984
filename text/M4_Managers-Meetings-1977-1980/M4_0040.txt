Boatd of Directors' Meeting Page Two
February 26, 1980

Howard's note of farewell to Mrs. Rettenmayer who has moved to
Olympia, Washington.

A letter of resignation from Board Director, Mrs. Herbert Moffitt,

was read. It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Sutro, Mrs. Forward)
to accept with regret the resignation of Mrs. Herbert Moffitt from the
Board of Directors of the Heritage.

ADMISSIONS

Mrs, Wert, reporting for Mrs. Scott, Chairman, told the Board that
three people were interviewed this month.

There are three vacancies for single occupancy, rooms 220, 407 and 105,
and double occupancy for apartment 248.

CRAFTS
At present, the crafts group is small. This may partly be due to an
exercise class scheduled just ahead of the crafts group. Some of the

members of the class may experience fatigue and find it difficult to
participate in the crafts group afterwards.

GARDEN

Mrs. Porter reported all is well with the garden. A lovely spring
garden is assured now that fertilizer has been purchased and distributed.

HEALTH

Mrs. Pope, Chairman, reported a "No Visitor" sign is posted at the
entrance of the Health Center. Both Residents and personnel have been
ill with severe colds and flu.

Mrs. Malcolm Watts is now a member of the Health Committee. Each
committee member adopts a certain number of patients in the Health Center
thus assuring each patient more individual attention.

HOUSE

Mrs. Abbott, Chairman, told the Board a letter had been sent to Mr.
Delfino asking him to be the interior decorator at the Heritage on

a continuing basis. The Board does not wish to impose upon him and
would like to put this on a financial basis. If Mr. Delfino does not
accept, the committee will seek another decorator.

Mrs. Stackhouse suggested the money from the sale of furniture should be
used to purchase a good piece of pieces of furniture. At present
proceeds for the sale of furniture must go into the general fund.

LIAISON

Mrs. McDaniel, Vice-President, reported a meeting was held with attorneys,
Mr. Howard and Mr, Stromberg, and Administrator, Mr. Stevens. Since the
Board of Trustees and the Board of Directors are satisfied with the
present functions of each Board, a board of final authority, conforming
to the 1980 Non-Profit law while stil continuing the functions of each
group, is sought. A final draft of revisions will be presented at

the annual meeting in May.

A letter from Mr. John S. Lynd, architect, was read enumerating
possible ways of expanding or the reorganization of available space
such as the use of sitting rooms. It was moved, seconded and carried
(Mes. Porter, Mrs. Sutro) that Mr. John S. Lynd not proceed with
anything further. (See Board minutes, June 1979, Liaison Committee)

Oop

Mrs. Logan, Chairman, told the Board at a recent meeting, the Resident
Council Committee again expressed approval of the food and service in
the dining room.

The Residents enjoyed a Valentine party on Valentine's Day and a
Mardi Gras Cocktail party February 19, 1980.
