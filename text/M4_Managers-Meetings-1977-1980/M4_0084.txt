MINUTES
The minutes were approved as mailed with the following addition to the Treasur-
er's Report: The Board approved the 1979 budget for $1,186,694 and Capital
Expenditures of $16,475.

POLICY COMMITTEE

The Personnel Manual will be completed by the end of this month. Mrs. McDaniel
asked Committee Chairmen to review with their committees the committee descrip-
tion before the April meeting.

The Resident Manual procedures will be brought up-to-date and Mrs. Moffitt
and Mrs. Gallatin will help the review. It was moved, seconded and carried
(Mrs. Porter, Mrs. Finley) to ask the President of the Resident Council,
Mrs. Girvin Wait, to assist.

A review is planned of the Health Center Committee description. Mrs. Pinkham
and Mrs. Avenali, of the Committee, will work with Mrs. McDaniel.

Chairmen of the Finance and Admissions Committee, Miss Balcom and Mrs. Scott,
are working on a revised formula for setting accommodation fees and monthly
charges for new admissions. They also will be working out a plan and formula
for implementing admissions under our special needs policy.

Suggestions on any of these topics are welcome--input is needed.
