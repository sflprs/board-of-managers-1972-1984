BOARD OF DIRECTORS' MEETING

September 25, 1979
CALL_TO ORDER

The regular meeting of the Board of Directors of the San Francisco

Protection and Relief Society was called to order at
10:40 a.m. on September 25, 1979 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco,
California by the President, Mrs. Knox Finley.

PRESENT

Miss Balcom, Mesdames Donlon, Ferguson, Finley, Forward, Gallatin,
Howard, Lampen, Logan, McDaniel, Pope, Porter, Scott, Stackhouse,

Vermilya and Wert. Mr. Roger Stevens, Administrator.
