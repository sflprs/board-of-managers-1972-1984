BOARD OF MANAGERS' MEETING
July 22, 1980
CALL_TO ORDER

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco
Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society was called to order at
10:30 a.m. on July 22, 1980 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco,
California by the President, Mrs. David J. McDaniel.

BOARD MEMBERS PRESENT

Miss Balcom, Mesdames Ferguson, Finley, Forward, Gallatin, Lampen,
Logan, McDaniel, Nishkian, Pinkham, Porter, Rhine, Scott, Sherman,
Stackhouse, Vermylia, Watts and Wert.

BOARD MEMBERS ABSENT

Mesdames Abbott, Avenali, Cox, Mitchum, Pope and Sutro.
STAFF PRESENT

Mr. Roger Stevens, Administrator

TREASURER'S REPORT

In the absence of Mrs. Avenali, Treasuzez, Mrs. Gallatin, a member
of the Finance Committee, presented the Treasurer's Report for the
month of June, 1980. (See distributed report)

There was some discussion concerning the inconsistent presentation of
budget figures. Mrs. McDaniel appointed a committee consisting of
Mrs. Avenali, Mrs. Mitchum, Miss Balcom and Mr. Stevens to meet with
the accountant and decide on a consistent format. It was moved,
seconded and carried to accept the Treasurer's Report.

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT
See distributed report.

The operatar of the Beauty Parlor has told Mr. Stevens that he is not
making enough money at the present rates charged for him to live on.
Mr. Stevens is working with him to try to arrive at a solution.

Mr. Angell is at present in Children's Hospital.

Mrs. Ferguson asked that Board Members be informed of meetings of
C.A.H.A. or other related organizations which might be of interest to
Board Members.

ADMISSIONS COMMITTEE

Mrs. Scott, acting for Mrs. Abbott, Chairman, distributed revised
copies of “Admissions Philosophy and Policies" and "Guidelines for
Admissions". It was moved, seconded and carried that they be approved
as corrected.

FINANCE COMMITTEE

Following discussion, it was moved, seconded and carried that there be
a general increase of 8.5% in Residents' monthly fees with those whose
contracts are related to their social security to be increased by 70%
of their July 1980 social security increase. Miss Balcom asked to be
recorded as opposed, preferring an increase of 8% only.

Letters concerning this will go out to the Residents on August 1st with
the raises to take place on September Ist. Mrs. McDaniel appointed a
committee consisting of either Mrs. Avenali or Mrs. Mitchum and Mrs.
Finley to review any requests by residents for special consideration of

their financial situation.
