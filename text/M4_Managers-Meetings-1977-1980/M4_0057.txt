Board of Directors’ Meeting Page Three
September 25, 1979

NEW BUSINESS
Mrs. Ferguson has given the Heritage three silver trays.

Mrs. Pope has given a monetary gift in memory of a close
friend.

It was moved, seconded and carried (Miss Balcom, Mrs. Pope)
to approve the gradual transfer of the pertinent records of
the Ladies' Protection and Relief Society (for the present)
up to 1970, to the California Historical Society for the
careful preservation which the Society affords. In turn,
the California Historical Society will provide copies of
the pertinent records for the Heritage. These records are
of great importance to the history of San Francisco.

Mrs. Ferguson will be in charge of this transfer of records
with Mrs. Logan and Mrs. Gallatin assisting.

A letter from our former accountant, Margaret Leinster, who
is now in England, was read.
