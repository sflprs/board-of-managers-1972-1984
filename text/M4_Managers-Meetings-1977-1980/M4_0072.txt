Board of Directors' Meeting

April 22 1979 Page Two

CORRESPONDING SECRETARY
Mrs. Finley read Mrs. Howard's Corresponding Secretary report.

Letters were written to Miss Helen Bridge urging her to remain on the Board of
Directors, Miss Ruth Dibble for her many hours devoted to The Heritage, Mrs. John
Parr Cox for the gift of the silver candelabra, Mr. Charles Ss. Sullivan, Jr. for

his donation in memory of Miss Georgina Ash, Mrs. Oscar Sutro and Mrs. Robert
Vermilya for speedy recoveries.

CRAFT
Mrs. Lampen told the Board the Residents who work in the Craft Shop need reasons

to crochet hangers make table mats, etc. Since these items are for sale a
suitable occasion to display these goods is necessary. '

GARDEN
Mrs. Porter regretfully reported one yew tree-,in front of The Heritage entrance
apparently is dead and the other yew tree is dying. These trees were planted

about 1920 and may be dying because of their age, but it was suggested the drought
may have hastened this loss.

Mrs. Porter will confer with the Department of Agriculture and also Mr. Kawamoto.
It may be necessary to pay a consultatation fee for these valuable trees.

HEALTH

No Committee report. However Mr. Stevens spoke of needing Board Members or
volunteers to accompany Residents to their medical appointments outside The Heritage.

ACTIVITIES
No report.
ADMISSIONS

Mrs. Scotty Chairman, told the Board Dr. and Mrs. John Pelkan will move into The
Heritage the end of May.

POLICY,
No report.
LIBRARY

Mrs. Pope reminded the Board Members that Mrs. Radcliffe, Resident Librarian, needs
magazines, books and paperbacks. She also mentioned the English novelist, Anthony
Trollope (1815-1852) is a favorite author of the Residents.

ORIENTATION

Mrs. Gallatin, Chairman, is planning an Orientation Meeting for the new Board
Members on Friday, June 15, 1979 at 10:30 a.m. Howevey Directors and Trustees
interested, are invited.

None,

None,

ADJOURNMENT
The meeting was adjourned at 12:10 p.m.
