Board of Managers' Meeting Page Two
July 22, 1980

It was moved by Miss Balcom and seconded by Mrs. Finley that any
recommendation by the Finance or other committee, which must be
referred to the Board of Trustees, be first submitted by the

committee chairman to the Board of Managers for the Board's expression
of approval or disapproval. The motion was carried.

CRAFTS :

Mrs. Lampen reported that everything is going well. The attendance
is very small.

FOOD

Mrs. Finley reported that the department is running smoothly. Several
special parties have been given for the Residents including a June
cocktatd. party, a party for the male residents, a Father's Day party

and a Fourth of July party. The Health Center was included in most
of these.

GARDEN

The Board concurred in an expenditure approved by the Finance Committee,
of $260.00 for garden furniture for the deck on the second floor of the
Morgan building.

Mrs. Porter announced that Mr. Scharff, a Resident, wants to give a
bird bath for the garden. Mrs. Porter will confer with Mr. Kawamoto,
our landscape architect as to its' placing.

HOUSE COMMITTEE

Mrs. Rhine and Mrs. Scott have been appointed co-chairmen. There
was no report.

LIBRARY COMMITTEE

No report.

RESIDENTS" COUNCIL

Mrs. McDaniel and Mrs. Finley attended the July meeting of the
Executive Committee of the Residents’ Council. They feel that this

is a valuable relationship to maintain.

ANNOUNCEMENTS

On July 30th there will be a luncheon honoring the Resident Volunteers.
John Killeen is planning a Gay Ninties Party for residents ninety years
old or older. The date is tentatively set for October 4th. Board’
Members and spouses will be invited to attend.

ADJOURNMENT

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 12:28 p.m.
