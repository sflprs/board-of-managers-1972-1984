HEALTH CARE COMMITTEE

Mrs. Leonards, Chairman, reported that she and Miss Cooper visited Our Lady's Home
on November 18th for the dedication of their new skilled nursing facility.

The progress which has been made on the long range plan for the Health Center was
summarized by Mrs. Leonards. To date this has involved meetings and interviews
with key staff and Health Care Committee members including Dr. Bennington, Dr.
Henderson and Mrs. William Nigh.

In conclusion, Mrs. Leonards reported on her recent visits to two skilled nursing
facilities in Philadelphia.

USE COMMITTEE
In the absence of Mrs. Scott, Chairman, Mrs. Hultquist, committee member, reported
that the painting of the exterior of the Perry Building and Health Center is now in

progress. Mr. Delfino did consult with the House Committee and recommended that
the existing rose color be used again.

The House Committee is planning to replace the water damaged carpet in the second
floor Morgan sitting room as soon as the adjoining roof is repaired.

Mrs. Hultquist stated that the residents are very pleased with the new Dining Room
carpet. There has, however, been some concern expressed by the residents about the

removal of the Hallway/Dining Room dividers. The House Committee is in the process
of evaluating whether the dividers are needed as a sound and draft barrier.
