Administrator. At the request of Mr. Morshead, a group of Trustees and
Trustee-Managers have reviewed the Bylaws relating to the position of
Administrator. They have recommended certain changes in language for
the purpose of clarifying the line of authority between the Board of
Trustees, the Board of Managers and the Administrator. These proposed
changes are being prepared to be presented to the Society and voted

on at the Annual Meeting on May 3rd. The job description for the
position of Administrator will reflect the intended clarifications.

Mrs. MeDaniel also reminded Committee Chairmen to review their current
job descriptions with their Committees before the first of May, and to
note in their annual reports any recommendations for changes or addit-
ions. Chairmen should also be sure that their files and other materials
are in order for turn-over to in-coming Chairmen in May. She stressed
that it will be of great importance for a smooth transition under a

new Administrator that all Board members are well informed about the
specific responsibilities and procedures of the Committees on which

they are serving.
