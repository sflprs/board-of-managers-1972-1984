BOARD OF MANAGERS' MEETING

November 27, 1984

CALL TO ORDER

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco Ladies’
Protection and Relief Society was called to order at 10:30 a.m. on November
27, 1984 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco, California by the Second Vice-
President, Mrs. Charles Hall.

BOARD MEMBERS PRESENT

Miss Copper and Mesdames Bates, Gallatin, Hall, Hall, Jr., Hiller, Hultquist,
Ilg, Kelly, Leonards, Mustacchi, Reinecke, Sherman and Stackhouse.

BO. MEMBERS ABSENT

Mesdames Abbott, Avenali, Dodge, Jordan, LeFevre, Miller, Pinkham, Scott and
Stark.

STAFF PRESENT

Mr. Edward Benedict, Administrator, and Ms. Marla Long, Administrative Assistant.

MINUTES

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Bates, Mrs. Mustacchi) that the minutes
of October 23, 1984 be accepted as mailed with the following correction under
"Health Care Committee": "She stated that Dr. Henderson, Dr. Bennington, Miss
Cooper and Mrs. William Nigh will be joining the Health Care Committee to assist
with the long range planning effort."

TREASURER'S REPORT

Mrs. Hall, Jr., Treasurer, presented the Report of the Finance Committee for the
month of October, 1984. (See distributed report). It was moved, seconded and
carried (Mrs. Stackhouse, Mrs. Gallatin) to accept the Treasurer's Report as

presented.

Mrs. Hall, Jr. presented the 1985 Budget to the Board of Managers for approval.
After discussion, it was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Hall, Jr., Mrs. Reinecke)

that the 1985 Operating Budget of $1,808,890.00 be approved as presented.

Mrs. Hall, Jr. presented the 1985 Capital Expense Budget for approval. It was
moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Hall, Jr., Mrs. Gallatin) that the 1985 Capital

Expense Budget of $41,900.00 be approved as presented.
