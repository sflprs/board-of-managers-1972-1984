HEALTH CARE COMMITTEE

Mrs. Abbott, Chairman, reported that the committee met with the architects from
Hertka & Knowles and they are proceeding with the plans for the modifications in
the Health Center. They should have them finalized by the first part of February.
Construction should begin soon after the plans have been finished, with completion
approximately 3 months later.

HOUSE COMMITTEE

Mrs. Scott, Chairman, reported that material is being purchased to make new
dining room chairback slip covers. Also the committee continues to research
carpeting for the dining room. There will be a meeting of the House Committee
on Friday, February 3rd at 1:30 p.m. at The Heritage.

Mrs. Reinecke requested that the House Committee look at the light fixture in
the small Board room.
