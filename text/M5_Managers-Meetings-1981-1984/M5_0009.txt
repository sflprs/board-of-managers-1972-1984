BOARD OF MANAGERS' MEETING
September 25, 1984

CALL _TO ORDER

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco Ladies'
Protection and Relief Society was called to order at 10:40 p.m. on September
25, 1984 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco, California by the President
Mrs. Frank Abbott III. ,

BOARD MEMBERS PRESENT

Miss Cooper and Mesdames Abbott, Dodge, Gallatin, Hall, Hall, Jr., Hiller,
Hultquist, Ilg, Kelly, LeFevre, Leonards, Miller, Reinecke, Scott, Sherman,
Stackhouse, and Stark.

BOARD MEMBERS ABSENT

Mesdames Avenali, Bates, Jordan, McDaniel, Mustachhi, Pinkham and Watts.

STAFF PRESENT

Mr. Edward Benedict, Administrator and Ms. Marla Long, Administrative Assistant.
MINUTES

of July 24, 1984 be accepted as mailed with the following correction under

CORRESPONDING SECRETARY

Mrs. Dodge, Corresponding Secretary, read the report of donations made since the
last meeting as follows: 1) Mr. Pryce Byrdsley, 1 single size mattress; 2) Mrs.

M. K. Swingle, book donation to the library; 3) Mrs. Bernice Springer, book

donation to the library; 4) Mrs. Ida Peterson, a color television, antenna,

wicker stool, bathroom chair, 2 arm chairs; 5) S. Harris Showroom, donation of
fabrics; 6) Mrs. Toni Mason, a hand knitted lap coverlet; 7) Mr. & Mrs. Walter
Neubauer, 1 arm chair; 8) Mr. & Mrs. John Wiley, impatiens plants;9) Miss Gail
Montgomery, 1 new mattress, box spring and frame; 10) Mrs. Bernice Springer,assorted
bed and bath linens; 11) Mrs. Marion Harwell, bathroom shelf and Mrs. Robert Sherman,
2 tickets for the symphony.

Mrs. Abbott read a letter of resignation from Mrs. Malcolm Watts. It was moved,
seconded and carried (Mrs. Abbott, Mrs. Miller) to ask Mrs. Dodge to write a letter
to Mrs. Watts accepting her resignation with regret.

TREASURER'S REPORT

Mrs. Hall, Jr., Treasurer, presented the Report of the Finance Committee for the
month of July, 1984. (See distributed report), It was moved, seconded and carried
(Mrs. Gallatin, Mrs. Hall) to accept the Treasurer's Report as presented.

Mrs. Hall, Jr., presented the Report of the Finance Committee for the month of
August, 1984. (See distributed report). It was moved, seconded and carried

(Mrs. Leonards, Mrs. Hultquist) to accept the Treasurer's Report as presented.

Miss Cooper suggested that the notes at the bottom of the Treasurer's Report be
footnoted with an asterisk. The Board thought that this was a good idea.
