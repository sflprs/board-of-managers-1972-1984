CORRESPONDING SECRETARY

In the absence of Mrs. Stark, Corresponding Secretary, Mrs. Hultquist
read the report of donations made since the last meeting as follows:
1) Mrs. Alice Von Briesen, a wool blanket and feather pillow; 2) Miss
Gail Montgomery, 1 Mirrow Wall Hanging, 1 hanging ceiling light and
1 bed frame; and 3) Mr. & Mrs. Walter Neubauer, two garden chairs.

Mrs. Hultquist read a letter of appreciation from Mrs. James Forward.

TREASURER'S REPORT

Miss Balcom, Treasurer, presented the Report of the Finance Committee
for the month of March 1984. (see distributed report). It was moved,
seconded and carried (Mrs. Stackhouse, Mrs. LeFevre) to accept the

Treasurer's Report as presented.

Miss Balcom presented the recommendation of the Finance Committee that
the new carpet for the Dining Room and adjoining hall areas be purchased
through Western Contractors at their bid of $12,500.00 pending review of
measurements taken. It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Pinkham,
Mrs. McDaniel) that this recommendation be approved subject to final

approval by the Board of Trustees.
