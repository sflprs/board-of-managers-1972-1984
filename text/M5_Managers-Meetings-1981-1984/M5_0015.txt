OTHER BUSINESS

Mrs. Abbott reported on the Residents! Executive Council meeting which was held
on Tuesday, July 10th. She noted that the residents are in need of donations
to be used as bingo prizes. Also it has been suggested that the speakers could
be better placed in the Dining Room. This was discussed, and it was determined
that the most effective change would be to have the podium more centrally
located so that the speaker could be better seen.
