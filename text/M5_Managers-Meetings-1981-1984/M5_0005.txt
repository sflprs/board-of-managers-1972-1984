BOARD OF MANAGERS' MEETING
October 23, 1984

GALL TO ORDER
The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco Ladies'
Protection and Relief Society was called to order at 10:40 a.m. on October
23, 1984 at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco, California by the President
Mrs. Frank Abbott, III. ,
BOARD MEMBERS PRESENT

Miss Cooper, and Mesdames Abbott, Gallatin, Hall, Hall, Jr., Hultquist, Kelly,
LeFevre, Leonards, McDaniel, Miller, Mustacchi and Pinkham.

BOARD MEMBERS ABSENT

Mesdames Avenali, Bates, Dodge, Hiller, Ilg, Jordan, Reinecke, Scott, Sherman,
Stackhouse and Stark.

STAFF PRESENT

Mr. Edward Benedict, Administrator and Ms. Marla Long, Administrative Assistant.

MINUTES

It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Gallatin, Mrs. Mustacchi) that the
minutes of Setpember 25, 1984 be accepted as mailed.

RRESPONDING SECRETARY

In the absence of Mrs. Dodge, Corresponding Secretary, Mrs. Kelly read the report
of donations made since the last meeting as follows: 1) Mrs. Olga Baxt, towels;

2) St. Luke's Church, needlepoint chair seat and arm covers; 3) Mrs. Dorothy Herron,
stainless steel set of pots and pans; 4) Dr. Karl Pelkan, book donation to the

library; 5)Mrs. Claudia Pogojeff, book donation to the library; 6) Miss Marie

Delege, book donation to the library; and 7) Mr. & Mrs. Walter Cantor, book

donation to the library.

TREASURER'S REPORT

Mrs. Hall, Jr., Treasurer, presented the Report of the Finance Committee for the
month of September, 1984. (See distributed report). It was moved, seconded and
carried (Mrs. Leonards, Mrs. McDaniel) to accept the Treasurer's Report as

presented.

Mrs. Hall presented the recommendation of the Finance Committee that the private
patient fees be raised to $60.00 a day for a four bed room and $65.00 a day for

a two bed room for all present and incoming private patients effective January 1,
1985. It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Hall, Jr., Mrs. Miller) to accept

the recommendation as presented.
