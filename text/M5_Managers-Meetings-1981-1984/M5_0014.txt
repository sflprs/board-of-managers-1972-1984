It was moved, seconded and carried (Mrs. Gallatin, Mrs. Hall) that the minutes
of June 26, 1984 be accepted as mailed with the following correction under

CORRESPONDING SECRETARY

Mrs. Dodge, Corresponding Secretary, read the report of donations made since
the last meeting as follows: 1) Mrs. Peter Avenali, 6 bottles of wine; 2) Ms.
Mitzi Bingo, miscellaneous items for bingo game prizes; 3) Mr. & Mrs. Robert
Klingel, a donation in memory of Constance Davidson; 4) Mrs. Olga Baxt, book
donation to the library; 5) Mrs. Olive Betsworth, book donation to the library;
and 6) Dr. Paul Kaufmann, reclining chair for the Health Center.
