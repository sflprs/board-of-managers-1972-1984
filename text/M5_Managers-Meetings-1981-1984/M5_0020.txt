MINUTES

Mrs. McDaniel corrected the transcription in the minutes of the previous Board
meeting of April 24, 1984 to read as follows: Past members of the Board of

Trustees and the Board of Managers are eligible to apply for admission to The i
Heritage at any time after their resignation. A year must elapse between the |
time of resignation and entrance into The Heritage as a resident. A prospective i |
resident may be shown a room, if one is available, ten months from the time of i
resignation. A member of the Advisory Council may apply for entrance to The
Heritage at any time after resigning from the council. Miss Balcom moved that
the minutes be approved as corrected and it was seconded and approved. i
