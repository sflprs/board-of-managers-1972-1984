CAHA ANNUAL MEETING

The Board of Managers of The Heritage was represented at CAHA's
Twenty-third annual meeting in Oakland on May fifth by Mrs. Frank
Abbott. III and Miss Laura Cooper. The theme "Tomorrow is Today"
was chosen to emphasize that issues must be addressed today for
tomorrow may be too late. Administrators, nurses, Board Members
and Residents were in attendance exemplifying the team concept
that gives the association its strength. The program was designed
to stimulate learning, improve management skills and motivate.

The General Session was to have been Dean Shelltler, Executive V.P.

of CAHA, speaking on "CAHA Trends". The address was instead de-
livered by Paul Fibbs of the Association of Associations that Mr.
Shelitter spoke of at the April Board Meeting. The CAHA organiza-
tion recently underwent a Program Evaluation and the recommendations
stemming from this were the basis of Mr. Fibbs' speech.

The Principal recommendation was, in keeping with the theme, to

look ahead and establish a mechanism to analyze objectives and
programs in light of today's challenges. His discussion was divided
into two parts and was general enough to apply to any organization.
The first point was the maintenance of credibility and the second
was the assumption of a leadership position by the association. His
ideas were well organized and presented, but not innovative.

There were two sessions for Trustees. The first on syndication was

not relevant to the management of The Heritage but the second,
presented by the Honorable Thomas M. Jenkins, Superior Court Judge.
San Mateo Co. (and former Heritage counsel) was interesting.

The judge has a long and varied history of involvement in boards

of many kinds and was a founder of both CAHA and the national assoc-
jation, AAHA. He spoke about the Major Tasks and Responsibilities of
