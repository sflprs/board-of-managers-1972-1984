# Board of Managers, 1972–1984

The women’s Board of Managers, which ran the Home: meeting minutes 1972–1984.

Part of the photographed records of the **San Francisco Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society** (Heritage on the Marina). Browse and search every series together at **https://sflprs.github.io**.

438 pages in 3 volumes (about 559 MB of images).

## About each volume

- **M3 Managers Meetings 1972–1977:** Spine: “Managers 1972–1977”. Most pages were photographed sideways.
- **M4 Managers Meetings 1977–1980:** Cover: “Board of Directors Minutes Regular Meetings 1977–1980”. The women’s Board of Managers used the name “Board of Directors” for a few years around 1977–80 (the late-1977 minutes in M3 already use it); the 1981–84 book is labelled “Board of Managers” again.
- **M5 Managers Meetings 1981–1984:** Cover: “Board of Managers Minutes Regular Meetings” (years not legible on the cover photo); dates 1981–1984 come from the minutes themselves. Most pages were photographed sideways.

## Folder layout

```
images/<volume>/   original photos, named <code>_<page>.jpg (e.g. M3_0001.jpg)
thumbs/<volume>/   small previews used by the viewer
text/<volume>/     machine-read (OCR) text of each page, same names, .txt
manifest.csv       one row per page: file names, original scan file, checksum, date found, notes
checksums-md5.txt  MD5 checksum of every image
```

Page numbers follow the order the photos were taken, which is not always the order of the book; some volumes were photographed newest-first or in two sessions (parts A and B in `manifest.csv`). The text is machine-read and contains errors; handwritten pages are not searchable.
